What to write about

  • Bugs or crashes — include your Android version, phone model, app version and what you were doing.
  • VPN problems — include the server you chose and the error you saw.
  • Feature requests and feedback.
  • Privacy or data questions — see our Privacy Policy and data deletion page first.
  • Reporting an inappropriate ad — include a screenshot if you can.
