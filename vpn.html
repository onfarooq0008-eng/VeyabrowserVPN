Built-in VPN

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Veya includes an optional VPN so you can hide your IP address from websites and encrypt your traffic on public Wi-Fi.

How it works

  • The VPN is off by default. It only starts when you open VPN in the app, choose a server and tap Connect.
  • Android asks for your approval the first time (the system “VPN connection request”). You can disconnect at any time from the app or from the notification.
  • Traffic is encrypted with WireGuard, using the official WireGuard Android tunnel library.
  • Browser traffic only mode sends only Veya’s traffic through the VPN. Other apps keep using your normal connection.
  • While connected, websites see the VPN server’s IP address instead of yours.

VPN service disclosure (Google Play)

Veya uses Android’s VpnService API. It is used for the app’s core, user-facing functionality: an optional VPN that creates a secure, encrypted tunnel between your device and a VPN server we operate.

  • Your traffic is encrypted and routed to the VPN server you select, so the sites you visit see the server’s IP address.
  • We do not use the VPN to collect or sell personal or sensitive user data, to show ads, to inject or replace ads or content in pages, or to track you across apps or sites.
  • We do not decrypt or modify the content of your HTTPS traffic.
  • The VPN starts only after your explicit action and Android’s permission prompt, and Android shows its key icon while it is active.

Please note

  • A VPN protects your connection between your phone and the VPN server. It does not make you anonymous: websites you log in to still know who you are, and a VPN cannot protect against malware or phishing.
  • Do not use the VPN for anything illegal. See our Terms of Use.
  • Speed and availability depend on server load and your network. Some services block VPN addresses.
