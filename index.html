Android browser

Browse fast. Browse private. Built-in VPN.

Veya is a lightweight Android browser with an ad & tracker blocker, isolated private tabs, a smart tab manager, a video player with floating picture-in-picture and a fast download manager — plus an optional built-in VPN.

Why people use Veya

Ad & tracker blocker

Blocks ads and trackers with the public EasyList and EasyPrivacy lists, so pages load faster and cleaner.

Built-in VPN

Optional WireGuard VPN you can switch on from inside the browser. Choose a server, or protect just the browser.

True private tabs

Private tabs run in a separate WebView profile: their cookies and site data never mix with normal tabs.

Advanced tab manager

Search tabs, group them (Work, Shopping…), swipe to close, restore recently closed tabs and your last session.

Video player & floating window

Play web videos in a built-in player and keep them playing in Android picture-in-picture while you use other apps.

Fast downloads

Multi-connection downloads with pause, resume and progress notifications, saved to a “Veya” folder.

Your data stays with you

Veya has no accounts and no analytics. Your bookmarks, history and settings stay on your device. The app shows ads from Yandex Mobile Ads after you start a download — details are in our Privacy Policy.
