Permissions

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Veya asks only for what a browser needs. Sensitive permissions are requested at the moment you use the related feature, never at install time.

PermissionWhy it is used
Internet, network stateLoad web pages, downloads, filter lists and ads, and show offline messages.
CameraOnly when you tap Scan QR code, or when you allow a website that asks to use your camera (for example a video call). Frames are not saved or sent to us.
Microphone, audio settingsOnly for voice search or when you allow a website to use your microphone. Audio is not recorded or sent to us by the app.
Location (approximate and precise)Only when you allow a website that asks for your location (for example maps). You choose each time.
NotificationsShow download progress and completion, and the VPN connection status.
Foreground service (data sync)Keep a download running while the app is in the background.
VPN service (BIND_VPN_SERVICE)Only when you connect the built-in VPN. Android shows its own confirmation first.
Storage (Android 9 and older only)Save downloaded files to your Downloads folder. Newer Android versions do not need this.
Advertising IDUsed by the Yandex ad SDK to show and measure ads. You can reset it or opt out in Android settings.

Permissions can be changed at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Veya → Permissions. See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.
