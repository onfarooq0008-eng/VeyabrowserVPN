Data & account deletion

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Veya has no user accounts and we do not store personal data about you on our own servers, so there is no account for us to delete. This page explains how to remove the data that does exist, and how to contact us about it.

1. Delete data stored on your device

  1. Open Veya and go to Menu → Clear data.
  2. Choose what to erase (history, cookies, cache, site data, download history, form data, saved permissions) and confirm.
  3. To remove everything the app stored, uninstall Veya, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Veya → Storage → Clear data.

Files you downloaded are in the “Veya” folder inside your Downloads folder. Delete them with your file manager, or open Menu → Downloads in the app and remove them there. Recently closed tabs and saved tabs are deleted together with your browsing data, or by turning off “Restore tabs after restart” in Settings.

2. VPN data

When you use the optional VPN, your device’s registration on the VPN control server (your public key and assigned tunnel address) is removed automatically when you disconnect. If you want us to confirm that nothing remains, email us (below) and tell us roughly when you used the VPN. Your private VPN key exists only on your device and is deleted when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

3. Advertising data

Ads are provided by Yandex Mobile Ads, which handles ad-related data under its own privacy policy. To limit it, reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Request deletion by email

If you believe we hold any data about you, email admin@fastvpnn.pp.ua with the subject “Data request”. We will reply as soon as we can, normally within 30 days.
