Support & FAQ

Quick answers to common questions. Still stuck? Contact us.

Do I need an account?

No. Veya has no sign-up or login. Your data stays on your device.

Is Veya free?

Yes. The app is free and supported by ads shown after you start a download.

How do I turn on the VPN?

Open the menu, tap VPN, choose a server and tap Connect. Approve Android’s VPN request the first time. Turn on “Browser traffic only” if you want only Veya to use the VPN.

The VPN will not connect. What can I do?

Check your internet connection, try another server, turn off any other VPN app, and make sure you approved Android’s VPN permission. If it still fails, email us with your Android version and the server name.

How do I use picture-in-picture?

Play a video, then tap the “Floating player” button that appears, or press Home while the video is full screen. Make sure picture-in-picture is allowed for Veya in Android settings and enabled in Settings → Advanced browser features. Some websites block PiP.

How do I restore my tabs after restarting?

Turn on Settings → Advanced browser features → Restore tabs after restart. Recently closed tabs are in the tab switcher under “Recently closed”.

How do I make a tab group?

Open the tab switcher, tap “+ New group” (the tab you are on is added), then long-press other tabs and choose “Add to group”.

Where are my downloads?

In the “Veya” folder inside your Downloads folder, and under Menu → Downloads in the app.

A website looks broken. What should I do?

Open the site settings for that page (from the menu) and turn off the ad blocker for that site, or try the desktop-site option. Report the site to us if it keeps failing.

How do I delete my data?

See Data & account deletion.

How do I report an inappropriate ad?

Email us a screenshot and what you were doing. We will report it to the ad network.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 (API 24) and newer. Features such as picture-in-picture need Android 8.0 or newer.
