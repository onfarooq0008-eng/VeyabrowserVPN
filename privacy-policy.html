Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026 · Applies to the Veya app (package veyabrowser.vpn) and this website.

This Privacy Policy explains how Veya (“the app”), developed by Umar Farooq (“we”, “us”), handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Summary
  • No account is needed. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number inside the app.
  • Your bookmarks, history, downloads list, tabs and settings stay on your device. We do not receive them.
  • The app shows ads from Yandex Mobile Ads after you start a download. Yandex may process device information such as your advertising ID and IP address.
  • The optional VPN sends a public key and your chosen server to our VPN control server when you connect. We do not use VPN data for ads and do not sell it.
  • We use no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and we do not sell your personal data.

1. Information stored on your device

To work as a browser, the app stores on your device: bookmarks, home-page shortcuts, browsing history (not saved in private tabs), the list of your downloads, per-site settings, app settings, cookies and website data kept by Android WebView, a counter of blocked requests, user scripts, elements you hid with “Mark as ad”, recently closed tabs, tab groups and (only if you turn on “Restore tabs”) the addresses, titles and groups of your open tabs so they can be restored. We do not receive or have access to this data. You can erase it with Menu → Clear data or by uninstalling the app.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the app ourselves and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The only server we operate that the app talks to is the VPN control server, and only if you use the VPN (see section 6). The third-party services the app contacts are described below.

3. Advertising (Yandex Mobile Ads)

The app uses the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK to show interstitial (full-screen) ads. Ads can appear after you start a download. To load, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK may collect and process information from your device, which can include:

  • Advertising ID (such as the Google Advertising ID) and device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and OS details (model, OS version, language, screen size, network type)
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, clicked or dismissed)

This information is processed by Yandex under Yandex’s privacy policy. We do not receive your personal data from Yandex; we only receive aggregated advertising performance reports. Your ad choices: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads). Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them less personalised.

4. Ad blocker filter lists

The ad blocker uses the public EasyList and EasyPrivacy filter lists. The app downloads them from easylist.to (or the mirror easylist-downloads.adblockplus.org) when you first use it and then about every 4 days. You can turn automatic updates off, or update manually, in Settings. Those servers can see your IP address and that a download was requested, like any website. No browsing data is sent.

5. Downloads, media and tabs

When you download a file the app connects directly to the website that hosts it, sending the cookies and the address of the page you came from (so the site allows the download), the same way a browser does. Files are saved in the “Veya” folder inside your Downloads folder. We do not upload or scan your files.

The built-in video player and the floating (picture-in-picture) player load the video directly from the website that provides it. The app detects videos on a page locally on your device; the video address is not sent to us. Open tabs, tab groups and recently closed tabs are stored only on your device.

6. Built-in VPN (optional)

The VPN is off until you turn it on. When you connect, the app creates a WireGuard key pair on your device (the private key never leaves it and is stored encrypted) and sends only the public key and the server you selected to our VPN control server (api.fastvpnn.pp.ua) to receive a tunnel address and the connection details. While connected, your internet traffic is encrypted and routed through the VPN server you selected, so websites see the server’s IP address instead of yours. The “Browser traffic only” option limits this to Veya. The app also contacts VPN servers to measure connection speed (ping). When you disconnect, your device’s registration is removed from the server.

We use Android’s VpnService API for this purpose only. We do not use the VPN to collect or sell personal data, to display or inject ads, or to track you, and we do not inspect or modify the content of your encrypted traffic. See Built-in VPN for the full disclosure.

7. Websites you visit

When you open a website your device connects directly to that site and to the third-party content it loads. That site receives standard information such as your IP address (or the VPN server’s IP address if the VPN is on) and browser details, and may set cookies under its own privacy policy. We do not see or control this. Veya offers tools that can reduce tracking: an ad and tracker blocker, third-party cookie blocking, optional Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control requests, private tabs and a clear-data-on-exit option. Private tabs do not save history, but they do not make you anonymous to websites, your network or your internet provider.

8. Search, translate and AI

Text you type in the address bar that is not a web address is sent to the search engine you select in Settings (Google by default, or Bing, DuckDuckGo, Ecosia, Brave, Yahoo or a custom one). If “Search suggestions” is on, the text you type is sent to your chosen search engine to fetch suggestions; this is never done in private tabs and can be turned off. Suggestions from your history and bookmarks are computed on your device only. The Translate feature opens the page through Google Translate. The AI feature works only when you tap it: it copies the page text and your request to your clipboard and opens the AI website you chose, where you paste it yourself; that website’s privacy policy applies.

9. Permissions

See the full list on the Permissions page. In short: Internet and network state (load pages, downloads, ads), Camera (only when you scan a QR code, or when a website you allow asks for it), Microphone and Location (only if a website you allow asks, or for voice search), Notifications (download and VPN status), Foreground service (keep downloads running), VPN (only when you connect), and Advertising ID (used by the ad SDK). Websites must ask first, and you decide each time.

10. Links that open in other apps

When a link can be opened by another app you have installed (for example YouTube) the app asks you whether to open it there. To do this it checks which installed apps handle that kind of link; this check stays on your device and is not collected.

11. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only (a) with Yandex Mobile Ads as described above, and (b) if required by law. VPN-related data is never shared with advertisers.

12. Data retention and your rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is little to retain or delete on our side. VPN registration data (your public key and assigned tunnel address) is removed when you disconnect. Data on your device is under your control: see Data & account deletion. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland, Brazil or California) you may have rights over personal data, such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and the right to object or opt out of personalised ads. Rights over data held by the ad provider are exercised with that provider and through your device settings. For anything related to the app, contact us at admin@fastvpnn.pp.ua and we will respond within a reasonable time.

13. International transfers

Yandex and the servers you connect to may be located in other countries. By using the app you understand that information handled by those providers can be processed outside your country, under their policies.

14. Children

Veya is a general-purpose browser and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. Because the app gives open access to the internet, parents should supervise use by minors. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

15. Security

The app blocks pages with invalid security certificates unless you choose to continue, only allows camera, microphone and location on secure (HTTPS) sites after you approve, and encrypts the VPN private key on your device. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page with its date above. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
