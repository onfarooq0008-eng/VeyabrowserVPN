About Veya

Veya is a fast, lightweight Android browser that puts privacy tools in one place: an ad blocker, isolated private tabs, a built-in VPN, a video player and a download manager.

It is built and maintained by Umar Farooq, an independent developer. The goal is simple: a browser that starts quickly, uses little memory and respects your privacy — without asking you to create an account.

Principles

  • Your data stays on your device. No accounts, no analytics.
  • Be honest about ads. The app is free and shows ads after downloads start. We explain exactly what that involves.
  • Respect websites. Veya does not bypass DRM or paywalls.
  • Keep it light. Fewer permissions, small size, smart memory use.
